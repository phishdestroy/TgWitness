How to check this record

Each collector signs its displayed snapshot and a checkpoint of its journal. Download successive snapshots to check signatures and continuity. The first key fingerprint needs a separate trusted reference.

Displayed text is a redacted presentation, signed separately from the original journal. A source hash identifies an original record; checking that original needs the corresponding evidence export. Current settings do not describe every earlier acquisition.

The page reads saved observations. It does not reconnect Telegram, recover unseen deletions or certify complete collection. Public-channel eligibility uses the latest saved source profile; its observation date is shown.
