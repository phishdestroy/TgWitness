Choose what to export

The scope and settings are fixed once collection starts. Every mode uses the same approved GitHub runner, signed journal and encrypted delivery.

Export settings

Secret chat

A @username, a phone number with + and the country code, or a person from this account's chats. Most people have no username: choose them from your chats or enter their number.

Secret chats have no history on Telegram's servers. Only what arrives while this runner is active is recorded: messages, files, timers, deletions, screenshot and read notices. You can write from the capture screen; every message you send is recorded too.

If Telegram limits this account (spam block), it can start secret chats only with mutual contacts. Add each other to contacts, or let the other side start the chat: incoming chats are accepted either way.

What the other side sees

Everything is saved first. By default the runner stays invisible: no read receipts, no typing, and self-destructing content is not opened for the other side, so their timers do not start. Viewing saved files in this panel never notifies anyone. Every notice you allow and send is recorded in the journal.

    Assessment details
    

    Takeout can require confirmation in an established Telegram session, including after phone, QR or two-step login. Telegram sets the wait, which may exceed the session length chosen for this runner, including login and approval. A new runner creates a new login and may need approval again, cross-run resume is unavailable. A persistent local export can resume the same session but does not carry GitHub attestation.
    

    The export covers the cloud chats available to the fresh session, private chats included when selected. The export adds Telegram Desktop compatible views of the signed journal. The archive records limits, failures and any incomplete acquisition.
